## 3. Summary Risk Posture

- **BPE-1.7 Trust Foundation Readiness:** All identity spoofing (`R10`, `R11`), PID-reuse/exit-race (`R12`, `R13`), filesystem alias (`R14`), and verification-window TOCTOU (`R15`) risks are **Mitigated** to **Low** residual risk within the verification primitives, backed by **239/239 passing tests** (including 84 adversarial tests from A2, B2, and C2) and **zero new NuGet dependencies**.
- **Remaining Pre-Production Gates:** **R16** (binding `QueryFullProcessImageNameW` -> `WindowsExecutableIdentityVerifier` inside a composite `IIdentityBoundaryVerifier`), **R5** (SQLCipher + DPAPI encrypted persistence), and **R6** (disposable Windows 11 VM enforcement validation) remain active gates before enabling live OS authorization or enforcement in BPE-1.8+.
